<div class="vision-container">
    <h2 class="section-label">Research Vision</h2>
    <p class="vision-quote">
        "The next generation of energy solutions will not come from the lab bench alone, nor from the plant floor, but from the synthesis of both."
    </p>
    <p class="vision-text">
        My work operates at the intersection of <strong>Condensed Matter Physics</strong> and <strong>Industrial Scale-Up</strong>. 
        As Scientist-B at <strong>BPCL Corporate R&D (CRDC)</strong>, I lead the <strong>alkaline water electrolyzer project</strong> for green hydrogen — 
        integrating industrial reality with advanced frameworks (Spintronics/DFT) across commercial OER/HER electrocatalysis, 
        H₂S valorization, and hydrogen combustion safety.
    </p>
</div>
