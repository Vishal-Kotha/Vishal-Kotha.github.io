Most fellowship applications are written in private, submitted in silence, and never spoken of again unless they succeed. I want to do the opposite. This is the first entry in **Fellowship Diaries** — a public log of my preparation for a **Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA)** postdoctoral fellowship.

## Why write it down where anyone can read it

Three reasons.

First, **it forces clarity.** The MSCA is scored on three axes — Excellence, Impact, and Implementation — and a proposal only works when you can state, in plain language, what your scientific problem is, why it matters to Europe, and exactly how you'll deliver. Writing for a reader who is *not* in my sub-field is the fastest way to find the sentences that don't yet make sense. My [Research Vision](/research-vision/) page was the first product of that discipline; this series is the running commentary.

Second, **the process is opaque, and it shouldn't be.** When I was a student, the mechanics of European fellowships felt like a locked room — supervisors, host institutions, work packages, secondments, the strange arithmetic of the evaluation form. Every early-career researcher I know has re-learned the same lessons from scratch. If documenting mine saves someone a wasted month, that's worth more than the small risk of showing my working.

Third, **science communication is itself part of the score.** MSCA reviewers look for researchers who engage the public and the next generation. A blog that explains, honestly, how a real proposal comes together is not a distraction from the application — it's evidence for it.

## What this series will cover

Over the coming entries I plan to write about:

- **Choosing a host and a research problem** that genuinely fit — not just chasing a famous name.
- **Turning a research vision into work packages** with deliverables and a timeline a reviewer can score.
- **Framing mobility and impact** — how my India → Israel → industry path becomes a strength rather than a footnote.
- **The honest parts:** rejections, rewrites, and the things I'd do differently.

## If you're on the same road

Whether you're a Master's student wondering what a postdoc fellowship even is, or a fellow researcher drafting your own MSCA, I'd like this to be useful to you. If a specific part of the process would help you to see worked through, [tell me](/contact/) — I'll write about it.
